How a bag gets made

Four stages, one drum

DRYING

Moisture out

Green beans lose surface water before any browning starts. Rushing this scorches the outside.

MAILLARD

Sugars brown

The bean's own sugars and amino acids react, building the body and most of the flavor.

CRACK

First crack

Steam pressure splits the bean. From here, every extra minute pushes the flavor darker.

DROP

Cool immediately

Beans hit a cooling tray the second they're dropped, so residual heat doesn't keep roasting them.

Beans ship the day after roasting.

No warehouse sitting around — your bag is roasted, sealed, and on a truck within 24 hours.
